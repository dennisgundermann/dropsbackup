How it works

A plan in two taps.

A plan is whatever you feel like doing right now, live on the map. Start your own or jump into one nearby. No planning required.

1

What are you doing?

Type an activity — coffee, a run, a beer. The more specific, the more people join.

2

Where are you?

Confirm your location and your plan goes live on the map right away. People nearby can ask to join — you decide who comes.

3

Show up. Meet.

Apple Maps guides you there. On arrival, Bluetooth confirms you’re together automatically — no check-in.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who can see my location?

Only participants of a running plan you’re part of. Outside a plan nobody knows where you are — there is no history others can look at.

How does arrival confirmation work?

When you arrive, Bluetooth automatically confirms that you’re there together. It counts towards your “Trusted” badge. No manual check-in needed.

What if I don’t show up?

If you say yes to a plan and don’t turn up, it counts against you — quietly, in the background. Only people who really show up earn the “Trusted” check mark.

What does dazu cost?

The app is free, with no ads and no premium locks on the core features. You need an iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Settings → Delete account. Your data is removed completely. Details are in the privacy policy (German).
