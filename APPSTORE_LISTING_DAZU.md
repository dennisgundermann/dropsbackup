## English

**Untertitel** (22/30)

Show up, skip the chat

**Werbetext** (112/170)

Who nearby is up to something right now? See it live on the map. Tap, go, done. No swiping, no group chat. Free.

**Schlüsselwörter** (89/100)

**Beschreibung** (1359/4000)

No swiping. No small-talk marathon. No likes. Just go.

dazu shows you live who nearby is up to something right now: coffee, a run, spikeball, after-work drinks. You see it on the map, tap “I’m in!” and join. Plans without the planning.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start a plan: type an activity, confirm your location. Your plan is live on the map instantly.
• Join one: browse plans near you, send a quick request, go.
• Meet: on arrival, Bluetooth confirms automatically that you’re there together. No check-in, no chat.

WHY DAZU
• Live, not “sometime”: the map shows what’s happening now.
• You decide who comes: decline requests, block people with one tap.
• Private from the start: your location is live only while a plan is running. Optionally, show up on the map delayed (20 minutes) and only roughly (rounded to about 500 m). Switch it off any time.
• Home-zone protection: dazu warns you when a plan close to your home would reveal where you live.
• Trusted check mark: show up when you say you will and you earn it. Counted quietly in the background, no points table, no pressure.
• Share a plan by link: friends see what it’s about first and join with one tap.
• Live Activity: your plan’s status on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Free, no ads. For everyone 18 and over. Sign in with Apple or Google.

Questions, ideas, love? dazuapp.com/support
